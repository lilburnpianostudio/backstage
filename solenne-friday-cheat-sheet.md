## 2. Five Questions That Unlock Everything
1. **"Tell me the story. Who's the business: you, a partner, an LLC?"** → owner, LLC status, who signs off.
2. **"When we're stuck between two options, who makes the final call?"** → single decision-maker.
3. **"Of the concepts, which logo and colors feel most like you?"** → lock lotus + sage/cream/gold or pick a direction (drafts A-D ready).
4. **"Walk me through the first product: bars, sizes, bottles, packaging?"** → real dimensions, quantities, soap-first scope.
5. **"What's in it, and what will you say it does?"** → ingredient list + claims ("cleans" vs. "nourishes") decide label rules.

*Bonus if time: business address for labels, target launch date, budget for print.*

## 4. Label Quick Reference
- **Cosmetics (FDA):** product identity · net weight (oz + metric) · ingredients, INCI names, descending order · business name + address · adverse reaction contact.
- **Pure soap** (cleans only, no skin claims) = looser rules (CPSC). **Skin claims** ("nourishing," "hydrating") = FDA cosmetic.
- Not legal advice; verify with a regulatory pro before print.
